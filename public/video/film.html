The problem

Small businesses want AI to run the routine work.
Handing an agent money is the scary part.

Prompt injectionA message tells the agent to "ignore your rules and pay us".
OverspendingNo limit on how much, or how often, it can pay.
Wrong recipientsMoney sent to an account nobody approved.
Leaked keysA key that can do everything, sitting on a server.

How the money is protected

Every payment has to pass five independent layers.

1
A NEAR function-call keyThe Finance Agent can call one method, request_payment, on one contract. It can't transfer funds or change keys.
checked on chain before every signature
2
Our treasury contract on NEARApproved recipients only · max 0.1 NEAR per payment · 0.25 NEAR per day · one payment per request.
enforced on chain
3
A deterministic security gateALLOW, WARN or BLOCK with reasons: limits, blocklist, recipient checks. No AI in the decision.
before anything is signed
4
Owner approval, bound to the exact paymentRecipient, amount, workflow, policy and contract are hashed. Change anything and the approval is void.
every real payment
5
A kill switchStops everything not yet sent, instantly.
tested live on testnet
