# M3.5 proposal · Discovery intelligence

Status: **Revised and approved 2026-11-06.** First approval: scope P1–P4, AC-1 to AC-16, Q1–Q8 as recommended, plus the owner's eight safeguards; P1–P4 implemented offline. **Revision (owner decision, same day): understanding-first discovery**: phase **P6**, AC-4, 5, 8, 11 and 13 revised, **AC-17 to AC-22 added**, decisions Q9–Q12 and three further safeguards (S9–S11). **P1–P4 and P6 implemented offline; awaiting owner review.** Not committed or pushed; no AI provider called. Results: [M3.5 report](poc-results/m3.5-report.md).

**Why the revision.** P1–P4 investigate a problem well once it is recognised, but the conversation was still driven by a list of fields. "Hello, I'm Isaac and I'm into real estate. I want to automate my chat replies to my clients." recorded only the goal and then asked *"What kind of business do you run?"*, which made the owner repeat himself. The consultant must **understand the whole message first, show what it understood, and investigate only the gaps that matter.**

Inserted between M3 and M4 by owner decision on 2026-11-06 (see the [decision log](README.md#decision-log-owner-decisions)). The problem analysis is in the [discovery diagnosis](discovery-reasoning-proposal.md). This document turns its four phases into the M3.5 scope.

**Why.** Discovery, analysis and agent recommendations are upstream of workflow execution. M4 must not execute workflows built on a misunderstood business problem. Today the consultant fills ten fields. It misfiles answers, drops the owner's stated causes, overwrites contradictions and ends on coverage (diagnosis RC1–RC8). M3.5 fixes the **architecture**, not the prompt wording.

## 1 · Scope

| Phase | Delivers | Est. |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 · Stop the harm** | Remove the "file the whole answer under the pending field" fallback. Keep causal clauses ("because", "since", "so"). Allow follow-up questions on a field that already has a value. A conflicting value raises a contradiction instead of overwriting. An asked-but-unanswered question no longer counts as resolved | ½ day |
| **P2 · Case file** | New reasoning state stored in the profile (`version: 2`): **facts** (typed slots), **claims** (the owner's view: symptoms, causal claims, goals, constraints), a **problem tree** (symptom → claimed cause → possible causes), a **question agenda** (purpose, priority, attempts, status), **contradictions**, and **reasoning notes**. The code-owned turn pipeline: interpret → update → choose the question → reply → readiness. The **ten profile fields are derived from the case file** as a compatible view. Existing conversations are migrated. The "What I understand" panel is updated | 1–1½ days |
| **P3 · Offline diagnostic playbooks** | Reviewed, deterministic playbooks: patterns, possible causes, one diagnostic question per cause, and answer classifiers. **Restaurant in depth:** overbuying/waste, running out, missed orders, low profit or cash, slow service. **Retail and salon lighter.** An honest fallback for anything unrecognised | 1 day |
| **P4 · Grounded analysis and agents** | Analysis as **cause chains**, with a basis on every item (owner-confirmed / supported by your answers / possible, not verified). "Measure first" items for unverified causes. **Agent recommendations driven by problems and their causes**, with reasons citing them. Optional cause-specific agent tasks in the catalogue | ½–1 day |
| **P6 · Understanding first** (revision) | **One-pass reading** of every message: owner's name, industry (about 15 industries), goals, problems, channels, tools, team, place, and the needs they signal. Anything the reader doesn't recognise is **kept verbatim and clarified, never discarded** (S9). The case file gains **owner**, **needs**, **inferences** (two kinds, kept apart: *implied by your words* and *typical for your industry*, S10) and **possible solutions** (being considered, never committed before the analysis). Questions are chosen by **materiality**: the gaps that would change a possible solution or a diagnosis, never something already stated or reasonably inferable. Readiness **by need, not coverage**. A cross-industry **client-enquiries** question set. Replies follow a reasoning framework (understand → suggest → ask what matters) expressed naturally, not a fixed template (S11) | 2 days |

**Timebox: 3 days (Nov 7–9), a hard boundary.** P1–P4 were completed on Nov 6. P6 fits in the remaining budget. Priority inside P6: the Isaac, Ada and reference-restaurant regression scenarios first; then the other introductions; retail and salon tuning is the first cut.

**Out of scope**
- **P5:** a live NEAR AI version of the new reasoning (separate approval).
- Any AI provider call, or a paid evaluation.
- New runnable industries.
- Changes to M3's workflow schema, template, validator or approval flow.
- New dependencies.
- Supabase.

## 2 · What stays the same

- **The five stages** (Introduce → Discover → Analyse → Confirm summary → Review team), the hero hand-off, one question per turn, "skip", the owner's message saved before any reply, "Try again", the editable summary and confirmation, team configuration and approval, and the "AI mode" badge ("Offline demo AI (rule-based)").
- **The ten profile fields stay available** (business name, industry, products & services, how customers buy, team & structure, daily operations, tools, suppliers, challenges, goals). They are now a *view* derived from the case file, used by the summary, the team proposal, M3's workflow generation, the workspace header and every existing API response.
- **Grounding rules:** every fact and claim cites a real owner message; owner edits win; nothing is invented.
- **Security:** owner text is untrusted data, the consultant has no tools, and the existing prompt-injection test is kept.
- **Restaurant is the only runnable scenario;** other industries stay Preview. Fictional demo data stays separate from the case file.

## 3 · Data model (sketch)

```ts
type CaseFile = {
  version: 2;
  facts: Record<FactSlot, Fact>;          // typed slots, e.g. business.name, menu.items, team.size,
                                          // channels, tools, suppliers, opening_hours
  claims: Claim[];                        // { id, kind: "symptom"|"causal"|"goal"|"constraint", text,
                                          //   status: "stated"|"confirmed"|"questioned", sources[] }
  problems: Problem[];                    // { id, symptom: claimId, claimedCause?: claimId,
                                          //   hypotheses: Hypothesis[], resolution }
  //   Hypothesis: { id, playbookCause, status: "open"|"supported"|"ruled_out"|"confirmed",
  //                 evidence[] }   // "confirmed" only from the owner's own words (code-enforced)
  agenda: AgendaItem[];                   // { id, purpose: { kind, target }, priority, asked: number,
                                          //   status: "open"|"answered"|"partly"|"declined"|"dropped" }
  contradictions: Contradiction[];        // { slot, a: {value, source}, b: {value, source},
                                          //   status: "open"|"resolved", resolution? }
  notes: ReasoningNote[];                 // owner-visible "why I asked / what I concluded", with evidence
  unclassified: string[];                 // message IDs not yet understood (clarified, never misfiled)
};
// fieldsView(caseFile) → the ten M2 profile fields (status confirmed / assumed / unknown), unchanged
// shape, so downstream code keeps working.

// P6 additions (all optional, so P1–P4 case files and migrated profiles stay valid):
type CaseFileP6 = {
  owner: { name: Fact | null };            // the owner's own name; outside the ten fields
  needs: Need[];                           // { id, capability, label, from: claimIds[], status: "stated"|"implied" }
  inferences: Inference[];                 // { id, kind: "implied"|"typical", statement, slot?, reason,
                                           //   from: sourceIds[], status: "inferred"|"confirmed"|"rejected" }
                                           //   "implied": follows from the owner's own words ("chat replies" →
                                           //   clients reach you by message). "typical": general industry knowledge
                                           //   ("enquiries in real estate are usually about availability, prices,
                                           //   viewings"), always labelled as typical, never as a fact about this
                                           //   business. Never inferred: quantities, team size, platform or tool
                                           //   names, prices, people's names.
  solutions: PossibleSolution[];           // { capability, agent, need, status: "considering"|"dropped", why }
  gaps: Gap[];                             // { key, for: needId|problemId, weight: "high"|"medium"|"low",
                                           //   status: "open"|"resolved"|"unknown" } — what would change a solution
  notes_unrecognised: Unrecognised[];      // { text, source } kept verbatim when the reader doesn't recognise it (S9)
};
```

**Migration.**
- Existing `version: 1` profiles convert: each known field becomes a fact with its status and sources.
- Text in *challenges* becomes a **stated symptom** with no causes yet.
- Conversations already past discovery keep their analysis and team, with a note that they were captured before M3.5.
- The migration is tested on a copy of the current dev store.

## 4 · Acceptance criteria

| # | Criterion | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| AC-1 | **Causal statements are kept.** "Our restaurant keeps losing money because we buy too much stock" records the symptom *losing money* and the owner's view *buying too much stock* as a **stated, unverified** cause, both cited | Unit test + panel check |
| AC-2 | **No misfiling.** An answer that doesn't answer the pending question is never stored in that field. It's recorded under its real meaning, or kept unclassified and clarified. The four misfiled examples from the diagnosis are regression tests | Unit tests |
| AC-3 | **Contradictions are raised, not overwritten.** "4 staff" then "just me" opens a contradiction and produces one clarifying question. "Actually…" is recorded as a correction and shown | Unit + route tests |
| AC-4 | **One question per turn, chosen by materiality.** Each question records which gap, cause, contradiction or fact it resolves and why that matters (*revised*) | Unit tests |
| AC-5 | **No repetition.** An answered question, or one with the same purpose, is never asked again. An unanswered one is rephrased at most once. "I don't know" and "skip" mark it unknown. **Nothing stated or reasonably inferable is asked**: each regression introduction has a list of forbidden questions (Isaac is never asked for his industry or what he sells) (*revised*) | Unit tests over scripted conversations |
| AC-6 | **Depth limits.** At most 3 follow-ups per problem, and at most 12 questions before offering to move on | Unit tests |
| AC-7 | **Facts, views and unknowns are separate.** The panel shows confirmed facts, *your view — not yet verified*, still unknown, and to resolve. A possible cause becomes *confirmed* only from the owner's own words, enforced by code | Unit tests + browser check |
| AC-8 | **Restaurant diagnosis.** For each of the five restaurant playbooks, a scripted conversation reaches an owner-confirmed or supported cause, or an explicit "cause unknown — measure first", within the limits. **A goal without a stated problem is investigated through the problem behind it** (cross-industry client-enquiries question set) (*revised*) | Scripted conversation tests |
| AC-9 | **Honest fallback.** An unrecognised problem gets "I don't recognise that yet; tell me more" and a request for the owner's own description. No cause is invented | Unit tests |
| AC-10 | **Explains its reasoning.** A question that tests an assumption includes a one-sentence reason. Reflections mention only recorded items | Unit tests (templates) |
| AC-11 | **Knows when to move on, by need not coverage.** Ready when the industry and at least one goal or problem are known, the high-weight gaps of each possible solution and each problem are resolved or marked unknown, and no contradiction is open; or at the question budget, with the unknowns listed. Products and services are not required when clear from context. A goal-led introduction answered cooperatively is ready in **5 questions or fewer**. "Analyse now" stays available (*revised*) | Unit + route tests |
| AC-12 | **Grounded analysis.** Each item carries its basis (owner-confirmed / supported / possible). Bottlenecks are cause chains. Unverified causes produce "measure first" items, not automation | Unit tests |
| AC-13 | **Grounded agents.** Each recommended agent cites the problem and cause, **or the owner's stated need**, it addresses, with their basis. No agent is justified only by a keyword, an inference or an unverified cause. The **reference restaurant conversation yields the Orchestrator + Order, Inventory, Procurement and Finance Agents**. Agents for non-restaurant businesses stay Preview (*revised*) | Unit + route tests |
| AC-14 | **Compatible.** The ten fields remain available to every existing consumer, existing conversations migrate, and **all 74 existing tests pass** | Full suite + migration test |
| AC-15 | **Offline and in scope.** No AI provider or network call (`fetch` stub), deterministic results, live adapter unchanged, restaurant the only runnable scenario, other industries Preview | Tests |
| AC-16 | **Quality bar.** Prompt-injection test kept; desktop and mobile in both themes; WCAG AA contrast; no horizontal overflow; `tsc`, `next build` and `git diff --check` pass; an M3.5 report with screenshots | Browser + CI checks |
| AC-17 | **One-pass understanding.** Every stated item in an introduction is recorded in the first turn, with its source. Regression introductions: **Isaac** (real estate, chat replies), **Ada** (bakery, hours answering WhatsApp orders), the **reference restaurant**, the salon with no-shows, a retail shop, a consultancy, and a message mixing a name, a goal, a problem and a correction | Regression tests |
| AC-18 | **Shows what it understood before asking.** The first reply greets the owner by name when given and restates the industry and the goal or problem in plain language, mentioning only recorded items. Later replies acknowledge what was new, without repeating summaries already given (S11) | Regression tests |
| AC-19 | **Inference kept separate.** Inferences are stored and shown apart from facts, with their reasons, in two kinds: *implied by your words* and *typical for your industry*. Isaac's "clients reach you by message" is implied, never confirmed; real-estate enquiry topics are shown as *typical*, never as facts about his business; no platform, quantity, team size or name is ever inferred. An inference becomes confirmed only from the owner's words and can be rejected (S10) | Unit + regression tests |
| AC-20 | **Needs and possible solutions.** Isaac yields the need *automated customer communication* and the Customer Messages Agent **being considered** (Preview), linked to that need. Nothing is proposed before the analysis; a possible solution can be dropped by later answers | Unit + regression tests |
| AC-21 | **Understanding updates on every message.** Volunteered information, a changed goal or a correction updates the case file and the panel in that turn, whatever question was pending | Scripted conversation tests |
| AC-22 | **Unfamiliar input is kept, not lost.** A statement the offline reader doesn't recognise is preserved verbatim with its source, acknowledged ("I didn't quite follow …"), and clarified with a question when it could matter; it is never discarded or misfiled because it isn't in the vocabulary (S9) | Unit tests |

## 5 · Tests (planned)

- **Unit:** extraction of causal clauses; the misfiling regressions; slots and contradiction detection; the question policy (priority, purpose fingerprints, limits, skip); hypothesis transitions (confirmed only from owner words); readiness; the fields view; migration.
- **Scripted conversations:** the five restaurant playbooks; a contradiction; an off-topic answer; an unknown problem; skip-heavy; the reference conversation for the demo team.

## 6 · What M3.5 hands to M4

1. A **confirmed problem statement and cause** (or explicit unknowns) for the business, with evidence.
2. **Agent recommendations** tied to those causes, which the owner approved in M2's team step.
3. Any **"measure first"** items and unresolved unknowns, carried forward and shown.
4. The **ten-field view**, so M3's workflow generation is unchanged.

M4's run view shows **which confirmed business problem the workflow addresses** (from the case file, labelled as your business information). This is kept visually separate from the **fictional demo data** the test run uses. See the [revised M4 proposal](m4-proposal.md#0--dependencies-on-m35).

**Safeguards added with the revision**
- **S9** The industry and phrase vocabulary is not a hard limit: unfamiliar descriptions are preserved verbatim and clarified, never discarded.
